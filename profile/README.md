<td width="50%" valign="top">

## KATCODE

<p>
<strong><i>Logic precedes code.</i></strong>
</p>

<img src="https://www.katcode.co.za/images/hackathon/collab.jpeg" width="100%" alt="Students collaborating with technology">


<br>

**KATCODE** is our flagship educational technology platform, designed to make software engineering principles engaging, accessible, and rooted in logical problem-solving.

Rather than focusing solely on syntax, KATCODE emphasizes the thinking, reasoning, and engineering principles that come before code.

### Platform Capabilities

- **Student & Project Management**  
  Tracking student execution, milestone progression, and code submissions.

- **Interactive Learning & Assessment**  
  Structured learning resources, quizzes, and automated evaluation.

- **Event Orchestration**  
  End-to-end infrastructure for student hackathons and technical events.

- **Engagement Systems**  
  Community-driven mechanisms designed to support consistent learner progression.

</td>
